Riftbound Deck Simulator

Simulates 10 games of your deck vs a chosen opponent and reports a winrate plus deck-optimization feedback.

Statistical / curve simulator - not a full rules engine. Runs entirely on this computer.

Import a deck from a text list

Paste a decklist - one card per line, like 1 Rengar, Pridestalker or 3x Inferna (section labels like Legend: or Battlefields: are fine too, they're just ignored). Every card's real Type/Energy/Power/Might/Domain is looked up automatically from the official card gallery data - you only need the name and the quantity to be right.
