        Tournament meta

        From Riot's official organized-play "Top Decks" articles: how many players brought each Legend, and the published lists (the Top 8 and the best list of each Legend).

        Legends played

        Day 1 is everyone who played; Day 2 the players who made the cut. A "Day 2 / Day 1" above 1× means the Legend did better than its share of the field.

        Published lists

        Tap a list to see it, simulate against it or save it as one of your opponent decks. "Text modeled" is how much of the list's card text the simulator plays - the rest only counts for its cost, Might and keywords.

        Test your deck against this field

        Plays games against the best-placed list of each of the event's most played Legends, then weighs each result by how many Day 2 players brought that Legend.

        Trend

        Each Legend's share of Day 2 over the last events (oldest first); the small number is how many Top 8 lists it had.

        Games

        Tap a game to edit it.

          Edit a deck

          Change card counts, add or remove cards, pick the Chosen Champion (it starts in the Champion Zone, playable any time), and fill in Tags (Remove:2, Buff:2, Draw:1, Shield:2) - the simplified effects the simulator uses for cards whose text it doesn't model. Changes are saved on this device.

              Import a deck from a text list

              Paste a decklist - one card per line, like 1 Rengar, Pridestalker or 3x Inferna. Section labels like Legend: or Battlefields: are fine too; a card under Champion: becomes the Chosen Champion and the Sideboard: is left out, as in Riot's published lists. Every card's real Type/Energy/Power/Might/Domain is looked up automatically from the official card gallery data - you only need the name and the quantity to be right. Imported decks are saved on this device only.
